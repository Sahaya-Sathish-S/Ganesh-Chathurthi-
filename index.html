🌺 About Ganesh Chathurthi

Ganesh Chathurthi marks the birth of Lord Ganesha — the beloved elephant-headed god of new beginnings — celebrated across India with devotion, music and community.

🕉️

Why We Celebrate

Ganesh Chathurthi honors the day Lord Ganesha was born to Shiva and Parvati. Families welcome him home as an idol, offer daily prayers, and after 1 to 11 days, bid him farewell through visarjan — a symbol of life's cycles of arrival and release.

🐘

Significance of Lord Ganesha

Known as the remover of obstacles and the god of beginnings, Ganesha represents wisdom, intellect and prosperity. He is invoked before any new venture, journey or celebration, as a blessing for clarity and success.

🪔

Traditional Celebrations

Homes and pandals install a Ganesha idol, perform daily pooja with flowers and durva grass, offer modaks, sing bhajans and aarti, and take part in vibrant processions before the final visarjan into water.
